Diabetes and your eyes: everything you need to know, explained simply. 👁️
சர்க்கரை நோயும் உங்கள் கண்களும்: முழுமையான எளிய விளக்கம்.

• The retina at the back of the eye is fed by very fine blood vessels; years of high sugar can weaken them so they leak or get blocked (diabetic retinopathy).
• NEI: it is the most common cause of vision loss in people with diabetes.
• Early stages often have no pain and vision can seem normal, which is why a yearly dilated eye exam matters.
• Warning signs: blurry or changing vision, dark spots or floating specks, poor night vision, faded colours.
• Diabetes can also change your glasses power, bring cataract earlier and raise glaucoma risk.
• Keep sugar, BP and cholesterol in control as your doctor advises, walk daily, and don't smoke.
• Sudden loss of vision or a curtain over your sight: see an eye specialist the same day.

Title: Is Thatha's vision just 'old age'? 👓 Senior eye checklist #Shorts
Description:
Thatha stopped reading the newspaper. It wasn't just old age.

• After 60, the eye's lens stiffens and can slowly turn cloudy (cataract).
• Eye pressure (glaucoma) can rise with no pain, so yearly eye tests matter.
• Keep sugar and blood pressure in control: both affect the retina.
• Bright lights near steps, corridors and bathrooms help prevent falls.
• Warning signs: night glare, faded colours, bumping into things, holding things very close.
• Sudden vision loss, a painful red eye or a dark shadow = see an eye specialist the same day.

Title: Can you read a number plate from 20 metres? 🚗 #Shorts
Description:
Can you read a car number plate from about 20 metres? Try this before your next drive.
வண்டி ஓட்டும் முன் உங்கள் பார்வையை சோதியுங்கள்.

• Self-check: in daylight, stand about 20 big steps from a parked car, cover one eye, read the plate, then swap eyes.
• At 50 km/h a car covers about 14 metres every second, so clear distance vision buys braking time.
• Distance blur often creeps in so slowly that you don't notice it.
• Starbursts or heavy glare around headlights can mean a power change or early cataract.
• Keep a clean pair of glasses in your current power in the vehicle, and clean the windscreen inside and out.
• Sudden blur, double vision or a dark patch: stop driving and see an eye specialist the same day.
• Eye test at our store is free.
