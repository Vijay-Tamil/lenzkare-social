Blurry vision in pregnancy? Common, but know when it is urgent. 🤰
கர்ப்ப காலத்தில் பார்வை மங்கலா?

• Hormones and extra body fluid can slightly change the shape of the cornea, so vision may blur a little.
• Dry, gritty eyes are common; contact lenses may feel uncomfortable.
• Mild changes usually settle after delivery and breastfeeding, so many experts suggest waiting before a new prescription.
• Urgent: sudden blur, flashing lights, spots or double vision, especially with headache or swelling, needs your doctor straight away.
• Rest your eyes, blink often, and switch to glasses if lenses hurt.
• Tell your doctor about any eye change, and book an eye test after delivery.
