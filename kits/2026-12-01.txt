Title: Eyes tired by 4 pm? Fix your desk setup 💻 #Shorts
Description:
Eyes tired and heavy by 4 pm? Your desk setup may be the reason.
கணினி திரை ஒரு கை நீளம் தூரத்தில் இருக்கட்டும்.

• Keep the screen about an arm's length away.
• Top of the screen at or just below eye level, so you look slightly down.
• Window to the side, not behind or in front of the screen; keep the room about as bright as the screen.
• Studies show we blink much less while looking at screens, which dries the eyes.
• 20-20-20: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds, and blink fully.
• Headaches or blur at work may mean you need glasses made for screen distance.

Title: 3 things bad sleep does to your eyes 😴 #Shorts
Description:
Slept badly? Your eyes feel it first. Here are 3 things poor sleep does to them.
தூக்கம் குறைந்தால் கண் வறட்சி, இமை துடிப்பு, கண் சோர்வு வரலாம்.

• During sleep, closed eyelids keep the eye surface moist and eye muscles rest.
• Poor sleep plus late screens can leave eyes dry, red and irritated.
• Eyelid twitching is often linked to tiredness, stress and too much caffeine, and usually settles with rest.
• Tired focusing muscles bring heavy eyes, blurred reading and evening headaches.
• Health experts say adults need about 7 or more hours of sleep a night.
• Phone away an hour before bed, dim lights, and a cool, clean, damp cloth on tired eyes.
• Twitching for weeks, a drooping eyelid or other face twitches: see a doctor.
