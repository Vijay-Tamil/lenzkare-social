Title: Suddenly seeing double? Don't ignore it ⚠️👀 #Shorts
Description:
Suddenly seeing two of everything? Don't brush it off.
திடீரென எல்லாம் இரண்டாகத் தெரிகிறதா? அலட்சியம் வேண்டாம்!

• Six tiny muscles move each eye, controlled by nerves from the brain.
• If the eyes stop lining up, the brain gets two pictures and you see double.
• Quick check: cover one eye. If the double image goes away, the eyes are misaligned.
• Sudden double vision can be linked to diabetes, high blood pressure, stroke or nerve problems.
• Go to a hospital immediately if it comes with severe headache, drooping eyelid, weakness, slurred speech or dizziness.
• Don't drive. Get checked the same day.

Title: Rainbow halos + eye pain? This is urgent 🌈⚠️ #Shorts
Description:
Rainbow rings around lights, with eye pain and headache? This can be an emergency.
விளக்கைச் சுற்றி வானவில் வளையம், கண் வலியா? உடனே கண் மருத்துவரிடம்!

• Fluid inside the eye normally drains out; in some people the drain can suddenly get blocked.
• Then eye pressure shoots up: this is acute angle-closure glaucoma.
• Sign 1: halos or rainbow rings around lights, with blurred vision.
• Sign 2: severe eye pain and headache, often on one side.
• Sign 3: red eye with nausea or vomiting (often mistaken for migraine or stomach upset).
• Without quick treatment it can cause permanent vision loss. See an eye specialist the same day.
• Risk is higher after 40 and in long-sighted people: get regular eye checks.
