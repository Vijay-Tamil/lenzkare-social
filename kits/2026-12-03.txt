• Lazy eye (amblyopia): if one eye is blurry or turned, the brain starts ignoring it.
• Treatment works best when started young, ideally before about age 7.
• Sign 1: often bumping into things or trouble catching a ball.
• Sign 2: tilting the head or closing one eye to look.
• Sign 3: an eye drifts in or out, or the child gets upset when one eye is covered.
• Often there are no signs at all, so get an eye test by age 3–5 and before school.
• An eye that turns needs an eye specialist soon.

VIDEO: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Vijay-Tamil/lenzkare-social/main/2026-12/yt/2026-12-03-colour-blindness.mp4

▶ YOUTUBE (Short)
Title: Red or green? Are you sure? 🔴🟢 Colour blindness facts #Shorts
Description:
Red or green? Are you sure? Some people really can't tell.
நிறக்குருடு பெண்களை விட ஆண்களுக்கு அதிகம்.

• The retina has three types of colour-sensing cells (cones) for red, green and blue.
• If one type works differently, some colours look alike.
• Studies show about 1 in 12 men, but only about 1 in 200 women, have colour vision deficiency.
• The common type passes down on the X chromosome; men have only one X.
• It's usually present from birth, doesn't get worse and is not blindness.
• A quick colour plate test during an eye test can find it: useful for school and some jobs.
• Colours suddenly looking faded, especially in one eye: see an eye specialist quickly.
