Glaucoma: the silent thief of sight, explained simply. 👁️
கிளாக்கோமா: சத்தமில்லாமல் பார்வையைத் திருடும் நோய்.

• The optic nerve is the cable that carries pictures from the eye to the brain; glaucoma damages it, often with raised eye pressure.
• Side vision fades first while central vision stays clear, so most people don't notice; NEI says about half of people with glaucoma don't know they have it.
• Open-angle glaucoma is the most common: slow, painless, no early signs.
• Angle-closure can be sudden: severe eye pain, red eye, headache, haloes, nausea. That is an emergency: go to an eye hospital immediately.
• Higher risk: family history, age over 40, diabetes, high minus or plus power, long-term steroid use.
• Lost vision cannot be restored, but early treatment helps protect what you have; never stop prescribed treatment on your own.
• Get eye pressure, optic nerve and side-vision checks regularly, especially after 40.

Title: Crusty eyelashes every morning? Clean your eyelids 👁️ #Shorts
Description:
Crusty lashes every morning? Your eyelids need cleaning too. 👁️
காலையில் இமையில் பொடுகு போல் படிகிறதா?

• Tiny oil glands line your eyelids. When they block, oil and flakes build up at the lash line (often called blepharitis).
• Eyelid inflammation is very common, and simple daily care keeps it in check.
• Warm compress: a clean cloth with warm (not hot) water on closed eyes for 5–10 minutes.
• Then gently wipe along the lash line with a clean, damp cotton pad. Always wash your hands first.
• Don't rub, pick the crusts, or sleep in eye makeup.
• See an eye specialist for pain, swelling, a lump, or blurred vision.
