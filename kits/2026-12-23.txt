Title: Gaming and kids' eyes: how close is too close? 🎮 #Shorts
Description:
Gaming and your child's eyes: how close is too close? 🎮
குழந்தைகள் கேம் விளையாடும் போது எவ்வளவு தூரம்?

• Tablet or phone: at least 30–40 cm away. TV: sit well back.
• Close focus for hours tires the eye's focusing muscle, and gamers blink less.
• Daily outdoor play in daylight helps lower the risk of short sight.
• Use 20-20-20: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
• Set a daily screen limit and no screens an hour before bed.
• Watch for squinting, eye rubbing, headaches or sitting too close, and book an eye test.

VIDEO: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Vijay-Tamil/lenzkare-social/main/2026-12/yt/2026-12-23-night-blindness-vitamin-a.mp4

▶ YOUTUBE (Short)
Title: Can't see well in the dark? Check your plate 🥕 #Shorts
Description:
Can't see well in dim light? The answer may be on your plate. 🥕
இருட்டில் பார்வை குறைவா? உணவு முக்கியம்.

• Your retina needs vitamin A to see in dim light.
• WHO: vitamin A deficiency is the leading preventable cause of blindness in children.
• Early signs: a child stumbles in dim light, or has very dry eyes.
• Eat the colours: carrot, papaya, mango, sweet potato, keerai, eggs and milk.
• No vitamin supplements without a doctor's advice; too much can harm.
• Night blindness has other causes too, so see an eye specialist if night vision gets worse.
