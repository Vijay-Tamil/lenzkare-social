Description:
Is it harmful to wear someone else's glasses? Here's the myth vs the fact.
மற்றவர் கண்ணாடியை போட்டால் கண் பாதிக்குமா?

• Myth: borrowed glasses permanently damage your eyes. Fact: no lasting damage, but they can cause headache, eye strain and blur.
• Every lens is made for one person's power AND the distance between their pupils (PD).
• Many people have a different power in each eye, or cylinder power for astigmatism, so a borrowed pair rarely matches.
• Ready-made reading glasses are the same power in both eyes; they're not a substitute for a proper eye test.
• Children should never use an elder's glasses; kids' eyes need their own checked power.
• If your own glasses feel blurry, your power may have changed. Get it rechecked.
