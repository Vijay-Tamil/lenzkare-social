Title: Minus or plus power? Short vs long sight 👓 #Shorts
Description:
-2.00 or +2.00: which one means short sight?
மைனஸ் பவர், பிளஸ் பவர்: எது கிட்டப்பார்வை, எது தூரப்பார்வை?

• Minus (−) power = short sight (myopia): near is clear, far is blurry
• In myopia the eyeball is a little too long, so light focuses in front of the retina
• Plus (+) power = long sight (hyperopia): the eye is a little short, so close work is a strain
• Short sight is becoming much more common in children worldwide
• Studies show daily outdoor time in daylight lowers children's risk
• Check children's eye power every year; if it rises fast, see an eye specialist

What do minus and plus mean on a glasses prescription? Minus power means short sight (myopia): near things are clear but far things like the board or road signs look blurry, because the eyeball is a little too long and light focuses in front of the retina. Plus power means long sight (hyperopia): the eye is a little short, so close work is a strain and a plus lens adds focusing power. Short sight is becoming more common in children around the world, and studies show that time outdoors in daylight lowers the risk. Send children out to play every day, check their eye power every year, and wear glasses as prescribed. If a child's power rises quickly, see an eye specialist. Title: What is PD on your glasses prescription? 👓 #Shorts
Description:
PD: two letters on your prescription that matter a lot.
கண்ணாடி சீட்டில் உள்ள PD என்றால் என்ன? அது ஏன் முக்கியம்?

• PD = pupillary distance: the gap between the centres of your two pupils, in millimetres
• Each lens's optical centre must sit right in front of your pupil
• A wrong PD can cause eye strain, headaches or a 'pulling' feeling, more so with higher powers and progressives
• Ordering online? Use the PD from your latest eye test; children's PD changes as they grow
