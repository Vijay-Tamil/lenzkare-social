• Glaucoma: risk is higher if a parent, brother or sister has it.
• Short sight: children of short-sighted parents are more likely to need glasses.
• Diabetes and BP in the family? Your retina needs regular checks.
• Genes are not destiny: early checks catch changes before you notice them.
• Ask elders about their eyes, write it down, and tell your eye doctor.
• Book an eye test for the whole family together.
