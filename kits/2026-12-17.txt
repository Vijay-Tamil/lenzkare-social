• Your brain is used to your old view; a new power, lens design or frame needs a little time.
• Normal for a few days: floor looks slightly tilted, objects look bigger/smaller, mild strain or slightly blurry edges.

• Tips: wear them all day, don't keep switching back to your old pair, and turn your head rather than just your eyes. Take stairs slowly at first.
• Come back if headaches, double vision or blur last beyond a week — the power or fitting may need a check.

• Sudden vision loss or eye pain needs an eye specialist urgently.

Title: Amma's eyes keep watering? It may be dryness! 💧 #Shorts
Description:
Elders' eyes keep watering? The cause may surprise you.
பெரியவர்களுக்கு கண்ணில் நீர் வடிகிறதா? காரணம் இதுவாக இருக்கலாம்

• Dry eye is a common cause of watering: an irritated, dry surface triggers floods of thin tears that don't protect well.
• Tears drain through a tiny duct into the nose; with age it can narrow or get blocked, so tears spill over.
• Eyelids can loosen or turn slightly in or out with age, so tears don't drain properly.
• Meanwhile: wipe gently with a clean tissue, blink often, and keep fan or AC air off the face.
• See an eye specialist urgently if there's pain, redness or swelling near the nose, pus, or blurred vision.
