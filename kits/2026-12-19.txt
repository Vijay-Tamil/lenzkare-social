Title: Party popper at a face? One pop can hurt an eye 🎉 #Shorts
Description:
Party poppers, crackers and corks: one pop can hurt an eye. 🎉
பார்ட்டி பாப்பர், பட்டாசு: கண்ணைக் காப்பாற்றுங்கள்!

• The front of the eye (cornea) is thin and delicate, so fast paper, plastic or a cork can scratch or bruise it.
• Point party poppers up and away from everyone, never at faces.
• Open bottles pointing away from people, with a towel over the cork.
• Kids and crackers: arm's length, safety glasses, a bucket of water, and an adult beside them.
• If something hits the eye, don't rub, press or try to pull out anything stuck. Cover it gently and go to an eye hospital.
• See an eye specialist urgently for pain, bleeding, blurred vision or any burn.

Title: AC all day and eyes burning? Here's why ❄️ #Shorts
Description:
AC all day and your eyes burn by evening? Here's why. ❄️
AC அறையில் கண் எரிச்சலா? காரணம் இதுதான்.

• AC pulls moisture from the air, so your tear layer dries faster and eyes feel gritty or tired.
• We blink much less when we stare at a screen, which adds to the dryness.
• Turn the AC vent and fan away from your face, and don't set the AC too cold.
• Follow 20-20-20: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds and blink fully.
• Drink enough water; a bowl of water or plants nearby can help the air.
• See an eye specialist if redness, pain or blurred vision doesn't go away.
