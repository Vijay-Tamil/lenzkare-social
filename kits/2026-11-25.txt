Title: Is Chennai sun hurting your eyes? ☀️😎 #Shorts
Description:
Chennai sun is strong all year, and its UV light can harm your eyes, not just your skin.
சென்னை வெயில் கண்ணையும் பாதிக்குமா? இதோ எளிய பாதுகாப்பு.

• UV rays are invisible; they reach the eye's surface and the lens inside it, and the effect adds up over years.
• Studies show long-term sun exposure raises the risk of cataract and of fleshy growths on the white of the eye.
• Choose sunglasses marked 'UV400' or '100% UV protection' - a darker lens is not always safer.
• Add a cap or wide hat and use shade around midday.
• Protect children too: their eyes let in more UV, and sand and water reflect extra light.
• Never look straight at the sun, even during an eclipse. A sudden blind spot or blur needs an eye specialist urgently.
