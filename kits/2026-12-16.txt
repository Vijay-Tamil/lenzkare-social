Title: Thyroid can affect your eyes! 3 signs to watch 👀 #Shorts
Description:
Thyroid problems can affect your eyes. Here are 3 signs to watch for.
தைராய்டு கண்களையும் பாதிக்கலாம் — 3 அறிகுறிகள்

• In thyroid eye disease, the immune system causes swelling in the muscles and tissue behind the eye.
• Sign 1: dry, gritty, red or watery eyes that don't settle.
• Sign 2: eyes look bigger or bulging, or the upper lid pulls back (a 'staring' look).
• Sign 3: double vision, or pain when moving the eyes.
• It's more common in women; smoking makes it worse. It can occur even when thyroid levels are under control.
• Don't smoke, wear sunglasses outdoors, and tell your doctor about any eye changes.
• See an eye specialist urgently if vision drops, colours look faded, or double vision starts suddenly.
