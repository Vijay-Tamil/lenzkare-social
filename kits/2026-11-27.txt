Title: When did you last blink? Try this 👁️ #Shorts
Description:
When did you last blink? On screens, probably not for a while.
கடைசியாக எப்போது கண் சிமிட்டினீர்கள்? இந்தப் பயிற்சியை முயற்சியுங்கள்.

• Each blink spreads a fresh layer of tears that keeps the eye's surface smooth, clear and comfortable.
• Studies show we blink much less while staring at screens, and many blinks only close half way.
• Blink exercise: close gently, pause 2 seconds, squeeze softly, open. Repeat 10 times, a few times a day.
• Every 20 minutes, look far away; a cool, clean cloth over closed eyes can feel soothing.
• Don't rub your eyes, don't let a fan or AC blow straight on your face, and don't use random eye drops without advice.
• Urgent: eye pain, redness, light sensitivity or blur that doesn't clear when you blink needs an eye specialist.

19:00 IST — Squint in kids: not 'lucky eyes'; early treatment matters
VIDEO: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Vijay-Tamil/lenzkare-social/main/2026-11/yt/2026-11-27-squint-in-kids.mp4

▶ YOUTUBE (Short)
Title: Squint = lucky eyes? Myth every parent believes 👧 #Shorts
Description:
Many families call a squint 'lucky eyes'. Here's what every parent should know.
ஒன்றரைக் கண் அதிர்ஷ்டமா? பெற்றோர் தெரிந்துகொள்ள வேண்டியது.

• In a squint, the two eyes don't point at the same spot, so the brain may start ignoring one eye.
• Myth: it's lucky, leave it. Fact: the ignored eye can become a lazy eye, with weak vision for life.
• Myth: children grow out of it. Fact: a true squint doesn't go away on its own; babies' eyes may wander only in the first few months.
• Treatment works best when started early: glasses, patching or surgery, as the eye specialist advises.
• Watch for an eye turning in or out, head tilting, or closing one eye in bright light - check family photos too.
• Urgent: a squint or double vision that appears suddenly, at any age, needs an eye specialist right away.
