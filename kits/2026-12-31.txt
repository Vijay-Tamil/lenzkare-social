Eyes dry after 40? Women, this one is for you. 👁️
40 வயதுக்கு மேல் கண் வறட்சியா?

• Hormones change around menopause, and tear glands and oil glands may make less.
• Dry eye is more common in women, and more common with age.
• Signs: burning, grittiness, watery eyes, and tired eyes by evening.
• Blink often on screens, keep fans and AC off your face, and drink enough water.
• See an eye specialist urgently for pain, strong redness, light sensitivity or a drop in vision.
• An eye test helps find the cause and the right care.
