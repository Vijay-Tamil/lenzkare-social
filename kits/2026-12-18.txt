Title: Itchy eyes? Don't rub! Do this instead 🙅 #Shorts
Description:
Itchy eyes? Don't rub them. Here's what to do instead.
கண் அரிக்கிறதா? தேய்க்காதீர்கள்!

• Dust, pollen, pet hair or mould trigger eye allergy; the eye releases histamine, causing itching, redness and watering.
• DON'T rub: it releases more histamine and worsens the itch; hard rubbing over time can harm the cornea.
• DO use a cool compress: a clean, cool, damp cloth over closed eyes for a few minutes.
• DO wash hands and face after coming in, change pillow covers often, keep windows closed on dusty days.
• Allergy usually affects both eyes. One red, painful eye is a different problem.
• Don't use leftover or borrowed eye drops.
• See an eye specialist urgently for pain, blurred vision, light sensitivity or thick discharge.
