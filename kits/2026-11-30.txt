Title: Chemical splash in the eye? Don't rub! 🚿 First aid #Shorts
Description:
Something splashed in your eye? Don't rub. Here's what to do in the first minute.
கண்ணில் ரசாயனம் பட்டால்: தேய்க்காதீர்கள், உடனே தண்ணீரில் கழுவுங்கள்.

• Rinse immediately with clean running water for at least 15 minutes, holding the eyelids open.
• Remove contact lenses if they come out easily.
• Don't rub. Don't put oil, milk or home remedies in the eye, and no drops unless a doctor says so.
• If something hit the eye or is stuck in it: don't press, don't pull it out, cover gently with a paper cup.
• After rinsing, go straight to an eye hospital or emergency, and take the chemical bottle with you.
• Prevention: cleaners out of children's reach, goggles for strong chemicals, and care with lime (sunnambu).
• General education only, not a substitute for emergency care.
