• A child's eye lens is clearer than an adult's, so more UV light reaches the back of the eye.
• UV damage adds up over a lifetime and is linked to cataract later in life.
• Clouds don't block UV. Sand, water and white walls reflect it back into the eyes.
• Choose sunglasses labelled UV400 / 100% UV protection, with a wide-brimmed hat.
• Avoid dark toy sunglasses without a UV filter: they make the pupil open wider.
• Plan outdoor play in shade between 10 am and 4 pm.
• School holidays are a good time for your child's yearly eye test.
