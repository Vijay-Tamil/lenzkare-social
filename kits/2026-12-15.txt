Do glasses make your eyes weaker? The myth vs the fact.
கண்ணாடி போட்டால் கண் பலவீனமாகுமா?

• Myth: wearing glasses makes your power keep going up. Fact: glasses only bend light to focus it on the retina; they don't change your eye.
• In children, power changes because the eyeball is still growing, with or without glasses.
• After about 40, the lens inside the eye stiffens (presbyopia), so near reading gets harder for everyone.
• Skipping glasses doesn't make eyes 'stronger' — it only causes squinting, strain and headaches.
• In young children, not wearing needed glasses can affect how vision develops.
• Get children's eyes checked every year, adults regularly as your eye doctor advises.

Description:
Straight lines look wavy? 3 signs of age-related macular degeneration to check after 50.
நேர் கோடுகள் வளைந்து தெரிகிறதா? 50 வயதுக்கு மேல் கவனியுங்கள்

• The macula is the centre of the retina; it gives sharp central vision for reading and faces.
• Sign 1: straight lines (door frames, tiles, text) look bent or wavy.
• Sign 2: a blurry or dark spot in the centre of vision. Sign 3: faces and small print get harder to see.
• Amsler grid check: hold a square grid at reading distance, wear your reading glasses, cover one eye, look at the centre dot. Note any wavy, blurred or missing lines. Repeat with the other eye.
• Studies show AMD is a leading cause of vision loss in older adults; smoking raises the risk.
• See an eye specialist urgently if lines suddenly turn wavy or a new dark spot appears.
