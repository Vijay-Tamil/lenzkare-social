Description:
Puffy eyes every morning? Here's why, and when it's urgent. ☀️
காலையில் கண் வீக்கமா?

• Lying flat all night lets fluid settle in the thin skin around the eyes.
• Common triggers: a salty dinner, late nights, crying, allergies and rubbing.
• Eyelid skin is among the thinnest skin on your body.
• Try a cool, clean cloth for 5 minutes, good sleep with the head slightly raised, and less salt at night.
• Don't rub: it makes swelling and itching worse.
• Urgent: pain, redness, fever, a change in vision, or one eye badly swollen. If your feet swell too, see a doctor.

Title: Your tears have 3 layers! Here's how they protect you 💧 #Shorts
Description:
Did you know your tears have 3 layers? 💧
கண்ணீருக்கு 3 அடுக்குகள் உள்ளன!

• Oil layer (top): from tiny glands on the lid edges; stops tears drying too fast.
• Water layer (middle): from the tear glands; washes away dust and germs.
• Mucus layer (bottom): helps tears spread and stick evenly on the eye.
• Every blink spreads them, and studies show we blink less when staring at screens.
• Protect your tears: blink fully, take 20-20-20 breaks, keep AC and fan air off your face, and drink water.
• Painful red eye or sudden blur needs an eye specialist urgently. For ongoing dryness, book an eye test.
