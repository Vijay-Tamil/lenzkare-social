Title: Phone in the dark makes you blind? Myth vs Fact 📱 #Shorts
Description:
Does using your phone in the dark make you go blind? Myth vs fact.
இருட்டில் போன் பார்த்தால் பார்வை போகுமா? உண்மை என்ன?

• Myth: a phone in a dark room permanently damages your eyes. There's no good evidence for that.
• Fact: it does cause eye strain: pupils keep adjusting and you blink less, so eyes feel dry, tired and achy.
• Bright screen light late at night can delay your body's sleep signal.
• Keep a room light on, lower the brightness and hold the phone at least 30 cm away.
• Children: fewer screens after dark and more outdoor time in daylight.
• Eye pain, blur that won't clear or daily headaches: get your eyes checked.
