Title: Stop sharing kajal! Eye makeup rules 💄 #Shorts
Description:
Sharing kajal or eyeliner? Here's why you should stop.
காஜலை மற்றவருடன் பகிர வேண்டாம்!

• Eyelids carry germs; a shared kajal pencil, eyeliner or mascara can spread eye infections.
• One person, one set: never share eye makeup, even with family.
• Eye experts advise replacing mascara about every 3 months; germs can grow inside the tube.
• Sharpen pencils before each use; apply liner outside the lash line, not on the inner rim (it can block oil glands).
• Never sleep in eye makeup; remove it gently every night with a clean cotton pad.
• If eyes turn red, painful, swollen or sticky: stop eye makeup, throw it away and see an eye doctor.
• General education only.

Title: Rainy days, red eyes? 3 monsoon eye rules ☔ #Shorts
Description:
Eye infections rise in the monsoon. 3 simple rules to protect your family.
மழைக்காலத்தில் கண் தொற்றிலிருந்து குடும்பத்தை காக்க 3 வழிகள்.

• Dirty rainwater, damp hands and crowded places help eye germs spread in the monsoon.
• Rule 1: wash hands with soap and water before touching your eyes.
• Rule 2: own towel, pillow and handkerchief for everyone; never share.
• Rule 3: keep rainwater out of your eyes; if it splashes in, rinse with clean water only.
• Eye flu spreads mostly through hands and shared things, not by looking at someone.
• See an eye specialist for pain, light sensitivity, blurred vision, or if it's not better in a few days.
• Don't use leftover or someone else's eye drops. General education only.
