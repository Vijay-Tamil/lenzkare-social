• A cataract is when the clear lens inside your eye slowly turns cloudy, mostly with age.
• Myth: wait till it ripens. Fact: surgery can be considered once it troubles your daily life.
• Myth: drops can dissolve it. Fact: no drops or diet can remove a cataract; only surgery can.
• Myth: weeks of bed rest. Fact: most people return to light daily activities within a few days, as their surgeon advises.
• WHO says cataract is a leading cause of blindness worldwide, yet it is treatable.
• See an eye specialist the same day for sudden vision loss, eye pain or redness.
