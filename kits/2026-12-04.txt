Description:
Eyelid jumping on its own? Usually harmless, but here's when to see a doctor.
கண் இமை தானாக துடிக்கிறதா? காரணம் இதோ.

• Eyelid twitching (myokymia) is a small eyelid muscle firing again and again; it usually settles by itself.
• Common triggers: poor sleep, stress, too much tea/coffee, long screen time, dry or tired eyes.
• Eye strain from glasses with the wrong power (or no glasses when you need them) can add to it.
• Try: 7–8 hours' sleep, less caffeine, screen breaks every 20 minutes, a warm compress on closed eyes.
• See a doctor if it lasts more than a few weeks, the eyelid closes fully, the eye is red/swollen, or other face muscles twitch.
• General education only, not a diagnosis.
