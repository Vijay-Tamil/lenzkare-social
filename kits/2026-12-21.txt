• The cornea is the eye's clear front window. In keratoconus it thins and bulges into a cone shape.
• Sign 1: power, especially cylinder, changes often.
• Sign 2: blurred, stretched or double vision, even with new glasses.
• Sign 3: glare and halos around lights, plus a strong eye-rubbing habit.
• It often starts in the teens. Found early, treatment can help slow it.
• Stop eye rubbing, get itchy allergies treated, and see an eye specialist for a cornea scan.
