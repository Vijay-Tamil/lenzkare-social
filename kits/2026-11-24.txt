Title: Headlights blinding you at night? 3 signs 🚗 #Shorts
Description:
Do oncoming headlights blind you at night? Watch for these 3 signs.
இரவில் எதிர் விளக்கு கண்ணைக் கூசுகிறதா? இந்த 3 அறிகுறிகளைக் கவனியுங்கள்.

• In the dark your pupil opens wide, so any haze in the eye or smudge on your glasses scatters light into glare.
• Sign 1: halos or starbursts around streetlights and headlights.
• Sign 2: your sight takes longer to clear after a bright light passes.
• Sign 3: it gets worse every year - night glare is a common early sign of cataract.
• Clean the windscreen inside and out, keep glasses spotless, and look at the left edge of the road, not into the lights.
• Yellow 'night driving' glasses don't help; lenses with anti-reflective coating cut reflections.
• Sudden blur or loss of vision? See an eye specialist the same day.

Title: Single vision, bifocal or progressive? Which one? 👓 #Shorts
Description:
Single vision, bifocal or progressive: which glasses do you actually need?
உங்களுக்கு எந்தக் கண்ணாடி சரி? எளிய விளக்கம்.

• After about 40, the lens inside the eye gets stiffer, so close-up focus gets harder (presbyopia).
• Single vision: one power across the lens - best when you need help for one distance only.
• Bifocal: two zones with a visible line - distance on top, reading below; the middle distance is missing.
• Progressive: no line - far, middle (computer, phone) and near flow smoothly in one lens.
• It can take a week or two to get used to progressives: wear them all day and turn your head, not just your eyes.
