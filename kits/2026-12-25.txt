Description:
Never stare at the sun, not even during an eclipse.
சூரியனை நேராகப் பார்க்காதீர்கள், கிரகணத்தின் போதும் கூட!

• Your eye's lens focuses sunlight onto the retina, like a magnifying glass.
• The retina has no pain nerves, so you won't feel the damage happening.
• Ordinary sunglasses are not safe for viewing the sun or an eclipse. Use certified solar viewers or a pinhole projector.
• Welding light without a proper mask can burn the front of the eye, causing painful, gritty eyes hours later.
• Supervise children during eclipses and around welding work.
• See an eye specialist urgently if you notice blur, a dark spot or bent lines after looking at the sun.

Title: Drivers: blurry signs at night? Read this 🚕👀 #Shorts
Description:
Auto, cab or bus driver? Blurry sign boards at night are a warning.
ஓட்டுநர்களுக்கு: வருடம் ஒரு முறை கண் பரிசோதனை அவசியம்!

• Vision often changes slowly, so drivers may not notice it getting worse.
• Driving depends heavily on your eyes; small blur can slow your reaction.
• Long hours, dust and AC air can dry the eyes and cause blurry, tired vision.
• Get an eye test every year, and keep windscreen and glasses clean.
• Take a short break every 2 hours on long drives.
• See an eye specialist urgently for sudden blur, double vision or loss of side vision.
