Title: Dust in your eye on the bike? Don't rub! 🏍️ #Shorts
Description:
Dust in your eye while riding? Don't rub. Do this instead.
பைக்கில் கண்ணில் தூசு? தேய்க்காதீர்கள்!

• The cornea (clear front of the eye) is delicate; rubbing drags grit across it and can scratch it.
• Pull over safely, blink a few times and rinse with clean water; tears often wash it out.
• Ride with a clear helmet visor down, or wear wraparound glasses (power glasses help too).
• Wind dries the eyes fast, which is why they water and sting on long rides.
• Don't dig it out with a finger, cloth corner or anything sharp.
• Urgent: pain, blurred vision, still feels stuck after rinsing, or anything that hit the eye at speed.
• Clear vision keeps you safe on the road: get your eyes tested.

Title: Read your glasses prescription: SPH, CYL, AXIS, ADD 👓 #Shorts
Description:
SPH, CYL, AXIS, ADD: what your glasses prescription really means.
உங்கள் கண்ணாடி மருந்துச்சீட்டை எளிதாக புரிந்துகொள்ளுங்கள்.

• SPH (sphere): your main power. Minus = short-sighted, plus = long-sighted.
• CYL (cylinder): corrects astigmatism, when the front of the eye is curved more like a rugby ball.
• AXIS: 0 to 180, the direction the cylinder power must sit in the lens.
• ADD: extra reading power, usually after 40, used in bifocal and progressive lenses.
• Your power can change: get your eyes checked every 1–2 years, or sooner if things look blurry.
• Eye test at our store is free; one free lens remake if the power doesn't suit after a LenzKare eye test.
