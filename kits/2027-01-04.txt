Title: Chilli in your eye? Kitchen eye safety 🌶️ #Shorts
Description:
Chilli in your eye? Kitchen eye safety: do's and don'ts. 🌶️
மிளகாய் கண்ணில் பட்டதா? என்ன செய்வது?

• Chilli and onion release chemicals that trigger pain nerves on the eye's surface.
• Wash hands with soap and water after cutting chillies, before touching your eyes.
• If it gets in: keep the eye open under clean running water for several minutes.
• Don't rub, and don't put ghee, milk or any home remedy in the eye.
• Chilled onions and a sharp knife mean fewer tears. Stand back from hot oil.
• See an eye specialist urgently for pain, redness or blurred vision after rinsing, or any hot-oil burn.

Headache after online class? Nila's tablet was too close, every evening. 💻
ஆன்லைன் வகுப்புக்குப் பின் தலைவலியா?

• Close screens make the focusing muscles work hard, and children blink less.
• Sit at a table with the screen at arm's length, top at eye level or a little below.
• Sit tall with back against the chair, feet on the floor, and room lights on.
• Follow 20-20-20, and make time for outdoor play every day.
• Studies show kids who spend more time outdoors are less likely to become short-sighted.
• Headaches, squinting or sitting close to the TV? Book an eye test.
