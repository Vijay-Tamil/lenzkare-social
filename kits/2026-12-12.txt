Simple screen rules for healthy young eyes. When children stare at screens up close for long, the focusing muscle inside the eye stays tight and they blink less, which leads to tired, dry eyes. Studies show that children who play outdoors in daylight are less likely to become short-sighted. Do: keep screens at arm's length, look at something far away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes, and switch screens off an hour before bed. Don't: give screens to babies and toddlers, let children use phones in a dark room, or keep screens on at meals. Make family screen-free time for books, games and play.
