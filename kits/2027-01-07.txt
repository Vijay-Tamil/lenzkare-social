Is your screen too bright? Match it to your room. 💻
திரை வெளிச்சம் அறைக்கு ஏற்றதா?

• Your eyes keep adjusting when the screen and the room don't match.
• Match the room: the screen should look like a sheet of paper in that light, not a lamp.
• Keep windows to the side, not behind or in front of the screen, to cut glare.
• Use dark text on a light background, and make the text bigger.
• Eye doctors say screen light doesn't damage eyes; strain comes from long staring and less blinking.
• Headaches or blur after screen time? Book an eye test.
