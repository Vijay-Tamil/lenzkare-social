Studying late for board exams? Protect your eyes while you study. 📚
பொதுத் தேர்வுக்கு இரவில் படிக்கிறீர்களா? கண்களைக் காப்பாற்றுங்கள்.

• Reading up close keeps a tiny focusing muscle working hard. Hours of it = tired eyes and headaches.
• Keep the room light on plus a desk lamp from the side, opposite your writing hand.
• Don't study in a dark room with only a bright screen.
• Book 35–40 cm away, sit upright, and every 20 minutes look far for 20 seconds.
• Time outdoors in daylight helps lower the risk of short sight in children.
• Sleep counts: tired eyes and a tired brain don't learn well. Blurry board or headaches? Get an eye test before exams.
