கண்ணாடி மருந்துச்சீட்டை எப்படி படிப்பது? சரியான லென்ஸ் எது?

• RE/OD = right eye, LE/OS = left eye.
• SPH is the main power: minus (−) means far is blurry, plus (+) means near is harder.
• CYL corrects astigmatism; AXIS (1–180) shows its direction, not its strength. ADD is extra plus power for reading, usually from the 40s.
• PD (pupillary distance) places each lens centre in front of your pupil; it must be measured correctly.
• Lens types: single vision (one distance), bifocal (far + near with a line), progressive (far, middle and near with no line).
• Options: anti-glare coating, photochromic (+₹1,000 on Fusion lenses), and thinner index for higher power (Fusion 1.50, 1.56, 1.60).

Title: One eyelid drooping? 3 signs not to ignore 👁️ #Shorts
Description:
Is one eyelid drooping? 3 signs you shouldn't ignore. 👁️
இமை கீழே தொங்குகிறதா?

• A small muscle lifts your eyelid; with age it can stretch and weaken (ptosis).
• Sign 1: tilting the chin up or raising the eyebrows to see.
• Sign 2: the lid covers part of the pupil, so the top of your view looks cut off.
• Sign 3: one eye looks smaller, often more noticeable by evening or in photos.
• In children, a droopy lid can block vision and lead to a lazy eye if not checked.
• Urgent: a lid that droops suddenly with double vision, severe headache or a weak face needs a hospital straight away. Most droopy eyelids can be treated.
