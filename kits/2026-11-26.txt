Title: Headache after screens? Quick quiz 🤯💻 #Shorts
Description:
Headache after hours on a screen or a book: is it your eyes, the screen, or both?
திரை பார்த்த பின் தலைவலி ஏன்? பதில் இதோ.

• Up close, a tiny focusing muscle in your eye works nonstop, and you blink less, so eyes dry out.
• Eye strain doesn't permanently damage your eyes, but even a small uncorrected power can make headaches worse.
• 20-20-20: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
• Keep the screen an arm's length away, slightly below eye level, with no window glare.
• Blink fully and often; read in good light.
• Urgent: a sudden severe headache with eye pain, redness, blurred vision or rainbow halos needs an eye specialist the same day.
