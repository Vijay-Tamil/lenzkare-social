Title: When should kids get eye tests? 4 key times 👶👦 #Shorts
Description:
Kids rarely say 'I can't see'. So when should you check their eyes?
குழந்தைகளுக்கு எப்போது கண் பரிசோதனை? 4 முக்கிய நேரங்கள்.

• At birth: a newborn's eyes should be checked before going home from hospital.
• Around 6-12 months: a check that both eyes move together and follow objects.
• Before school (3-5 years): when lazy eye and squint are easiest to treat.
• Every year in school: a child's power can change quickly, especially with lots of screen time.
• Most vision problems in children cause no pain; watch for sitting close to the TV, squinting, head tilting or eye rubbing.
• Urgent: a white glow in the pupil in photos, or an eye that suddenly turns, needs an eye specialist right away.
