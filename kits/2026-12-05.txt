Title: Red eye: wait or worry? 3 warning signs 🚨 #Shorts
Description:
Red eye? Most settle, but these 3 signs mean see an eye specialist today.
கண் சிவப்பு: இந்த 3 அறிகுறிகள் இருந்தால் இன்றே மருத்துவரிடம்.

• Red eye happens when tiny blood vessels on the white of the eye swell: dust, dryness, allergy or infection.
• Mild redness with itching or watering and clear vision often settles in a few days.
• Warning sign 1: real eye pain (not just itch or grittiness).
• Warning sign 2: light sensitivity, when normal light hurts.
• Warning sign 3: blurred vision that doesn't clear with blinking. Also urgent: injury or chemical splash (rinse with clean water first).
• Contact lens wearers: remove lenses and get checked quickly if the eye turns red and sore.
• Don't rub, wash hands often, don't share towels. General education only.

19:00 IST — Stye: warm compress, don't squeeze, see a doctor if it spreads
VIDEO: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Vijay-Tamil/lenzkare-social/main/2026-12/yt/2026-12-05-stye-care.mp4

Title: Stye on your eyelid? Don't squeeze it! 🚫 #Shorts
Description:
Painful bump on your eyelid? Do this, not that.
இமையில் கட்டி? அழுத்தி பிழியாதீர்கள்!

• A stye is a blocked, infected oil gland near the lash line.
• Do: clean warm compress on the closed eye, about 10 minutes, 3–4 times a day.
• Do: wash hands before touching your eyes; keep the eyelid gently clean.
• Don't: squeeze or pop it, as it can push the infection deeper and spread it.
• Don't: wear eye makeup or contact lenses until it heals; replace old eye makeup.
• See a doctor if swelling spreads to the eyelid or cheek, the eye swells shut, vision changes, or it isn't better in about a week.
• Most styes settle in about a week. General education only.
