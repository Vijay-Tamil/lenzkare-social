Title: One eye weaker than the other? It's common 👁️ #Shorts
Description:
One eye weaker than the other? Different power in each eye is common. 👁️
ஒரு கண் மட்டும் பலவீனமா?

• Each eye grows a little differently in length and shape, so the power can differ.
• You may not notice it, because the stronger eye quietly does the work.
• In children, if one eye stays blurry, the brain may ignore it (lazy eye).
• Cover one eye at a time and compare. Never borrow someone else's glasses.
• Sudden loss of vision in one eye: see an eye specialist the same day.
• Book an eye test for the whole family.
