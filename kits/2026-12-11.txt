Title: That puff of air at the eye test? 💨 Here's what it checks #Shorts
Description:
That puff of air during an eye test: what does it actually check?
கண் பரிசோதனையில் காற்று ஊதும் சோதனை எதற்கு? கண் அழுத்தம்!

• The 'puff' test measures the pressure inside your eye; painless, and nothing touches your eye
• Your eye makes and drains a clear fluid; if it drains too slowly, pressure can harm the optic nerve
• Raised pressure is linked to glaucoma, which often has no pain and no early signs
• Lost side vision from glaucoma cannot be brought back, so early checks matter
• Higher risk: over 40, family history, diabetes, high short sight
• Urgent: sudden severe eye pain, red eye, headache with vomiting or halos around lights
